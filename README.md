Divinum is a 2D action-adventure RPG developed and published by Sigil Games. It released on August 28, 2026, for Windows and macOS. The game combines Metroidvania-style exploration with fast-paced mod-and-slash combat.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Divinum**. This trainer provides optional control over combat resources and progression-related systems in Divinum. It is designed around the game's real mechanics, including health, Mana, Fury, combo building, two-weapon combat, skills, Runes, and exploration.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to practice Divinum's combat system, test different Runes and skills, explore the island more freely, or reduce difficulty during story and boss encounters.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents Fjør's health from decreasing during exploration and combat.

- **Infinite Fury** — Maintains the Fury meter so powerful skills and Fury-based attacks can be used more often.
- **Unlimited Combo Timer** — Prevents the combo chain from expiring while attacks are being practiced or extended.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Allows attacks to defeat regular enemies quickly.
- **Infinite Skill Uses** — Removes resource restrictions from equipped combat skills.
- **No Damage** — Stops incoming attacks from damaging Fjør while enabled.
- **Quick Challenge Practice** — Helps players rehearse weapon swaps, parries, blocks, projectile deflections, and combo routes in combat challenges.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate movement-related options only after gaining control of Fjør. Use the trainer to practice jumps, dodges, dashes, launches, and aerial positioning. Disable invulnerability when you want to rehearse normal defensive timing.

### Tools & Inventory

Divinum's progression includes collectible Runes, Life Shards, Mana Shards, skills, and equipment choices that influence combat. Use the trainer to test different loadouts and resource situations, but avoid changing progression values during item pickups, Rune changes, or save transitions.

### World & Environment

Divinum's island contains forests, caves, catacombs, sacred ruins, hidden secrets, bosses, and skill trials. Explore normally when possible, and use survival options to investigate dangerous areas or practice routes without repeated deaths.

### Combat & Weapons

Divinum centers on two primary weapons, weapon switching, ground attacks, launchers, aerial juggles, dodges, blocks, parries, projectile deflection, skills, Fury, and combo maintenance. Infinite Health and Mana are useful for learning attack patterns, while Unlimited Combo Timer helps practice extended strings and weapon-swap routes. Disable damage-related options before finishing bosses or challenge objectives if you want the intended results.
